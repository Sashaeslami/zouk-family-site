👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 About family members

Small on purpose. Close on purpose.

150people maximum
100%application only

Our goal is to have a balanced number of leads and follows, and we will be more strict with invites as we get closer to the limit.

Criteria for acceptance

💃 Experienced Zouk dancers

No beginners (people who have less than 6 months of dancing).

Why? This retreat is centered around Zouk dancing, and since we won't be having any beginner-level classes, beginners may feel out of place. We want everyone to feel comfortable that every dance will be an amazing opportunity for a unique journey with someone else.

🙌 Active participation and contribution

This is a community-led event. We all show up, pitch in and co-create it together.

⭐ Bonus: recommendation by past attendees

A warm word from someone who has been part of the family goes a long way.

💜 Cultural values

How we treat each other.

01

Safety

Emotional & physical safety, as well as adherence to our covid safety policy, is our number one priority during these times.

02

Radical Inclusion & Integration

In a world that is filled with divisions and social segregation, “Us vs. Them”, we strive to welcome, accept, and learn from our differences in thought, opinions and beliefs, as long as they can be in line with the values of this event.

03

Curiosity & Kindness

When seeing different viewpoints or ideas, see if you can face them with some kindness first, and then maybe some curiosity and benefit of the doubt, to go beyond the surface.

04

Initiative

Taking initiative to resolve any issues or implement any ideas that you directly or indirectly become aware of. It comes from “caring” and making an immediate action.

05

Extreme Participation

“We believe that transformative change, whether in the individual or in society, can occur only through the medium of deeply personal participation. We achieve being through doing. Everyone is invited to work. Everyone is invited to play. We make the world real through actions that open the heart.” Burning Man

06

Communal Effort

Not only is this event only made possible by communal cooperation and collaboration, it thrives to become a unique magical experience when many hearts and minds can build and construct with the help of each other.

07

Gifting

Our souls expand by gifting. A gift is unconditional. The value of a gift is unconditional. Gifting does not contemplate a return or an exchange for something of equal value.

🚫 Anti-values

  • Segregation & Division
  • Hate Speech
  • Consumption without Participation
  • Transactional Norms

No selling, buying, bartering, or conditional gifting is permitted between the members for the duration of this event.

👷🏽‍♀️ Your gift & contribution

Everyone brings something.

This is a community-led project inspired by events such as Burning Man, where the attendees take the majority of the roles of co-creating the event.

As a member of this event, it's expected that we all contribute in some sort of way.

In addition to the teamwork contributions, we all have wonderful gifts within us. How can we share some of those with each other? It can be as little or as big as we want.

  • After July 1st, any amount paid for a pass is non-refundable & non-transferable.

    • The total price of the pass might be different for next year and you will be accountable for the difference.
    • You have until November 1st to postpone your pass to next year.
      • If you do not postpone your pass until the deadline and you don't show up to the event by the check-in deadline, all of your payment is forfeited and you will not receive any refund whatsoever. Your room spot will also not be given to your roommate to have a bigger room. It is just forfeited.
      • We will fully incur all the costs related to your attendance beyond this date, so unfortunately there will be no refunds due to medical or any other urgent circumstances.
    • Passes can only be postponed for up to 1 year in the future, otherwise all payments will be considered forfeited. Meaning you cannot postpone 2 editions in a row.
    • If there are no future editions, your postponed amount is forfeited.
    • If you have incurred any sort of late fees because of non-responsiveness to our follow-ups, that amount will be deducted as well. (You may not have incurred any late fees, but we're just mentioning our general policy, because follow-ups are costly for us.) 🫢🤨😇
